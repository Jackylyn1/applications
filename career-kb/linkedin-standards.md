# LinkedIn Profile Standards

Channel-specific rules for Jacqueline Urban's LinkedIn profile. Inherits the
universal branding (`general-standards.md`) and voice (`communication-rules.md`);
for LinkedIn *posts*, also follow `social-media-standards.md`. Source:
`Professional_Branding_Standards.pdf` (Group B).

## Goal
- Position her as an experienced software engineer **transitioning naturally into AI engineering and solution architecture**.
- Roles to signal: AI Software Developer, AI Consultant, Solution Architect, Software Architect.

## Core positioning
- Communicate that she is an **end-to-end engineer** who **bridges business and technology**, **continuously learns**, **focuses on optimisation**, **uses AI as an engineering tool**, and is **growing into architectural responsibility**.

## AI positioning
- Show that she knows **where AI creates value**, **evaluates tools critically**, **optimises workflows**, **learns rapidly**, and **integrates AI into software engineering** (rather than treating AI as a separate skill).

## Overall profile impression
- "This engineer has a clear direction and is evolving naturally toward architecture and AI."
