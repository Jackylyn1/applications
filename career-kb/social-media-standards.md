# Social Media Content Standards

Channel-specific rules for social-media content (including LinkedIn posts).
Inherits the universal branding (`general-standards.md`) and voice
(`communication-rules.md`). Source: `Professional_Branding_Standards.pdf` (Group C).

## Storytelling
- Prefer personal experiences, practical lessons and real engineering examples over pure explanations or abstract theory.

## Hooks
- Open with curiosity; give readers a reason to continue.

## Value
- Communicate **one clear insight per post**.

## Simplicity
- Keep the core message understandable; remove unnecessary technical detail.

## Examples
- Use concrete situations instead of abstract explanations.

## Brevity
- Default to concise posts where every sentence adds value.

## Images
- Images should reinforce the story and communicate part of the message themselves.

## Tone
- Avoid AI clichés, clickbait, overly dramatic wording and empty motivation. Aim for thoughtful, practical, technically credible, authentic and experience-driven communication.
