## Cover Letter Standards
- Sound like an experienced software engineer.
- Style: authentic, confident, concise, friendly and solution-oriented.
- Structure: ~40% company / their problem, ~40% how you solve their problems, ~20% about you.
- Reference the company or job posting in the introduction.
- Mention technologies only when relevant.
- Avoid AI clichés and exaggeration.
- Never invent experience.
- Generate the final cover letter as **PDF**.
