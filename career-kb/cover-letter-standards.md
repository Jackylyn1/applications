## Cover Letter Standards

Cover-letter-specific structure only. Voice/tone → `communication-rules.md`;
fact source, language/register matching, email swap and PDF output →
`application-standards.md`; positioning → `general-standards.md`.

- **Structure by weight:** ~40% the company / their problem, ~40% how she solves it, ~20% about her.
- **Flow:** (1) open with the company's problem or mission; (2) connect her experience to that need; (3) support with one or two concrete project examples; (4) close by explaining why she wants to contribute — not by flattering the company.
- Reference the specific company or job posting in the **introduction**.
- **One page.**
- Mention technologies only when relevant.
